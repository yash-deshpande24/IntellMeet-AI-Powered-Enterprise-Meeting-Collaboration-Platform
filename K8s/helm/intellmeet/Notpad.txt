IntellMeet has been installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Check the pods:
  kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

Open the app (two terminals, keep both open):
  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/backend 5000:5000
  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/frontend 8080:80

Then browse to http://localhost:8080
